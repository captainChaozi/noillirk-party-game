# One answer, not a list: how Noillirk reads what you type

Source: [How to Play Noillirk](https://noillirk.pro/how-to-play) · Scoring: [Noillirk scoring explained](https://noillirk.pro/scoring) · Play: [Noillirk.pro](https://noillirk.pro/)

Each Noillirk prompt gives you 15 seconds to type the answer most people would give. New players lose points by typing the wrong *shape* of answer rather than the wrong idea.

## Type one clear example

- "A list of alternatives is not a single answer." Typing `banana, cinnamon` will not be read as two guesses.
- Use a clear English answer. Do not rely on automatic translation.
- Specificity can matter: "keys" and "car keys" can differ when the prompt makes that distinction important.
- Plurals, typos and synonyms are not accepted unconditionally.

## Rejected vs accepted

| Outcome | What it means | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| Rejected | Did not fit the category or a restriction, or the wording was not recognised | Try a different single answer while time remains |
| Accepted | Valid, scored 4 to 100 | Locked; it cannot be swapped for a higher score |
| Checking / pending | Verdict in progress or delayed | Wait; do not submit something else |

## Submitting

- Keyboard: Enter or click **Climb**.
- Phone: tap the up-arrow beside the answer field.
- Leave a moment for the answer to reach the server. A timely received answer can finish judging after the timer.

## The demo board

On the site's oatmeal-topping demo, "banana" earns 100 and "raisins" earns 16. Both fit; they sit in different tiers. Speed adds nothing, so use the seconds to pick the most common answer, not the first one.
