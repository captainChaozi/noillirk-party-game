# Hosting a Noillirk round for a group

I'm Chaozi, the developer of [Noillirk](https://noillirk.pro/). A short checklist for running it with friends, a stream chat or a remote team:

1. Share the link https://noillirk.pro/ in your group chat.
2. Everyone joins on their own device; one round is 7 questions, 15 seconds each.
3. Remind people: the goal is the *most common* answer, not the cleverest one.
4. After each question, talk about surprising results - that's where the fun is.
5. Play 2-3 rounds, then switch games before energy drops.

For streamers: read the question aloud and let chat vote on what they think the crowd will say before you answer.
