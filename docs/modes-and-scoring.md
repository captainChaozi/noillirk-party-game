# Noillirk modes and scoring

Companion notes for [Noillirk](https://noillirk.pro/), a free browser party guessing game where you score by matching what most people would answer.

## Modes
| Mode | What it is | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| [Classic](https://noillirk.pro/classic) | A fresh seven-question game | A short break |
| [Daily](https://noillirk.pro/daily) | Same seven questions for the UTC date | Comparing with friends |
| [Endless](https://noillirk.pro/endless) | Keep going with three balloon lives; Sky High restores one | Long sessions |
| [Multiplayer](https://noillirk.pro/multiplayer) | Live room | Friends online together |

Friends playing later can receive a challenge created from a completed Classic or Daily result with no pending answers.

## Scoring basics
- Each answer window is 15 seconds; type one clear answer.
- Answers that fit the prompt land in different scoring tiers. In the site's oatmeal-topping demonstration, banana earns 100 points and raisins earns 16.
- Speed adds no points.
- Rejected answers can be replaced while time remains; accepted answers are locked.
- After question seven, the flight log lets you compare answers and report a scoring issue.
