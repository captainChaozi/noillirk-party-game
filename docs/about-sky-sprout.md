# Sky Sprout and the Noillirk.pro art

Who the little explorer is and where the game's art comes from, summarised from the [Noillirk.pro](https://noillirk.pro/) home and [about page](https://noillirk.pro/about) (checked 2026-11-02).

## Sky Sprout

A small seed with a coral scarf, a satchel and a very big sky ahead. Each familiar answer you give lifts Sky Sprout higher through pixel worlds, from **Sprout Landing** (a tiny launch basket) upward. The [Sky Passport](https://noillirk.pro/worlds) lists 52 destinations.

## Art and animation

- The character, environments and animated world were created specifically for Noillirk.pro with **ChatGPT-assisted artwork and original animation**.
- Destinations and landmarks are original.

## Questions and boards

- Questions and answer rankings are created for this game.
