# Noillirk - the party game where you guess what the crowd says

I'm Chaozi, an indie developer, and **[Noillirk](https://noillirk.pro/)** is my free multiplayer party guessing game.

## How it works
- You get a question like "Name something you'd find in a kitchen drawer".
- Everyone answers; you score by predicting what *most people* would answer.
- A round is 7 questions, 15 seconds each - fast enough for a quick break.

## Good for
- Friends and game nights
- Parties and family gatherings
- Streams and watch parties
- Icebreakers for classes and remote teams

## Play
Free in the browser: <https://noillirk.pro/>

## Tips
1. Go with the obvious answer, not the clever one.
2. Short single-word answers match more often.
3. Don't overthink - 15 seconds is short.
